# Presentation rules

Status: consolidated owner decisions, updated16 November2026. All sizes are Figma pixels on a1920×1080 canvas. Read EXCEPTIONS.md for deliberate local variants.

## Typography and hierarchy

Use **Reckless Standard M VF** only. Preserve original logos as artwork; do not replace them with the presentation font.

| Role | Size | Style | Case / line height |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Main slide title |48|Medium|UPPER /105%|
| Section or panel heading |32|Medium|UPPER /105%|
| Card or column heading |28|Medium|UPPER /102%|
| Proper name in a heading |28|Medium|Preserve typed proper-name case /102%|
| Subtitle / lead directly under H1 |28|Regular lead; Medium subtitle where intended|Sentence /115%|
| Main body / card description |22|Regular|Sentence /115%|
| Small supporting text / short list rows |20|Regular|120%|
| Footnote / chart axis / map legend |16|Regular|120%|
| Chapter display |120|Medium or existing Regular composition|Preserve approved composition|
| Closing brand display |80|Medium|Brand artwork/text composition|
| Closing tagline |48|Regular|Preserve approved composition|

Role determines size, not whether text happens to contain digits. A numeric card heading remains a card heading. Peer headings of the same role use the same size: Guest Demographics and Second Home Demand must match.

Main headings retain the established width, approximately1320px maximum for a normal one-line H1; split-slide titles fit their own section. Do not stretch a title across the entire slide to avoid wrapping. Use logical two-line breaks, normally no three-line H1. Three lines require a recorded exception. Keep names and number-unit pairs intact; avoid stranded articles/prepositions. The lead fits at28 in at most two lines by adjusting available width rather than shrinking it.

In a sibling row, heading slots and line treatment align; descriptions start on one common guide. Separate heading+subtitle from body using a distinct Auto Layout group and a visibly larger inter-group gap. Do not give subtitle and body identical visual hierarchy.

A ±2px fit adjustment is an allowed, **logged** float, not permission to mix peer sizes. Do not normalize owner-authored exceptions automatically.

## Numbers and emphasis

- Dominant figures:72 Medium. Unit words/letters such as Nights, ADR, m, bn:56 Regular. Secondary figure tier:40; use Medium for an emphasized numeric value, Regular for subordinate fragments.
- Percent signs use the same size as their digits, including secondary tiers.
- Labels supporting dominant figures:28 Regular. The general20px supporting-text role does not override this.
- Important numbers can use terracotta; do not highlight every word.
- Center both value and caption inside equivalent metric cells, horizontally and vertically. Equal-width cells, equal inner spacing, common value/caption guides.
- Separate value and caption into stable slots; a two-line caption must not push its figure above neighboring figures.
- Chart labels and figures must clear the graph/ring. Inspect the actual image after resizing; equal visual weight does not justify overlap.
- Separate figures from unit letters/words with a nonbreaking space (U+00A0): `$339 m`, `129,203 m²`, `7.7 Nights`, `251 ADR`. The unit must not stick to the number or wrap alone. Currency prefixes and percent signs remain attached: `$339`, `70%`. Check rendered spacing as well as text content. The gate flags missing gaps as UNITSPACE. [Owner16 November2026, slide31]
- Examples: +70%; 81%; ~$251 ADR / average daily rate; 7.7 Nights / average length of stay; $339 m.
- Keep superscript references and square-meter notation. Preserve range punctuation and arrows. Presentation prose uses em dashes; do not replace numeric ranges with em dashes.
- Do not invent missing figures or rewrite approved copy. Keep Pulse Bay Wellness & Social Hub; the old Beach Club name is obsolete.
